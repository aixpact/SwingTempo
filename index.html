Why one tempo

Good players take close to the same time from takeaway to impact with a driver as with a wedge, and their backswing lasts about three times their downswing. A fixed rhythm takes timing out of the equation, so the swing gets more repeatable.

What the figure shows

The blue arc is the wrist hinge between the lead arm and the shaft: set by the top, held into the downswing as lag, released through impact. The green line is the pelvis. The bar under the feet is the weight split. Hips start forward before the arms finish going back.

How to practise

Study the positions at ¼× first, then swing with the beats at 1×. Start with half swings at 2 : 1, then full swings at 3 : 1. The positions are a model of a typical good swing, not a measurement of yours.
